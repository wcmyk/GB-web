About the shop

The shop doesn't look like much. The work speaks for itself.

Galbraith Professional Dry Cleaners sits on E Galbraith Rd near Deer Park, across from the Dillonvale shopping center. It's a small storefront — no flashy signage, no gimmicks — just careful, hands-on work from someone who's been doing this a long time.

Bring in something that matters — a decades-old dress pulled out of storage, a suit with a stain you've given up on, a gown you're not ready to part with — and you'll get a straight answer. If it can be saved, it will be. If there's any doubt, you'll hear about it upfront, no empty promises.

  • Family-run, hands-on approach to every garment
  • Honest assessments before any work begins
  • Specialists in difficult stains and delicate fabrics

What we do

Services

Every piece is inspected, treated on its own terms, and pressed with care. We take the time it takes to do it right — that's the whole approach.

Dry Cleaning & Pressing

Shirts, suits, and everyday garments cleaned thoroughly and finished with a crisp, professional press.

Wedding Gown & Delicates

Gentle, detailed care for gowns, lace, and heirloom pieces — including garments that have been stored for years.

Leather & Suede Care

Specialized cleaning and conditioning for leather and suede jackets, coats, and accessories.

Alterations & Repairs

Hems, resizing, and careful mending done by hand, so the fit and the fabric are both respected.

Tough Stains, Taken Seriously

Old stains, discoloration, garments pulled from storage after years — we won't promise a miracle, but we will take a close look and tell you honestly whether it can be saved.

We focus on getting every garment done right, not fast — we no longer offer one-hour service, so each piece gets the full time it needs.
